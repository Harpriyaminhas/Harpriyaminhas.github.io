Email Google Scholar ORCID GitHub LinkedIn

I am a computational materials scientist with a background in chemistry. I develop AI and machine-learning methods for materials discovery. My work combines machine-learning interatomic potentials, explainable models and generative design with electronic-structure theory. The aim is faster, more reliable and more interpretable materials simulations. I apply these methods to energy conversion, clean hydrogen and fusion materials.

At NUS, I work with Prof. Shyue Ping Ong in the AI Foundry programme. I develop foundation potentials for high-entropy alloys used in fusion and aerospace. I received my Ph.D. from IIT Indore with Prof. Biswarup Pathak as a Prime Minister's Research Fellow. My thesis developed machine-learning potentials and bonding descriptors for thermal transport and thermoelectrics.

Feel free to email me about collaborations, data or code.
